Polinex is a Fabric client for Hypixel SkyBlock. Version 1.5 has 56 macros, 27 helpers, 12 failsafe detectors, a route editor, HUDs and a JavaScript/TypeScript scripting API, and runs on Minecraft 26.1, 26.1.1, 26.1.2, 26.2 and 26.3.

Macros share one pathfinder, one route system and one set of failsafes, so a new macro works the way the last one did. The Scheduler chains them into sessions with breaks.

### [Mining](https://polinexclient.com/mining-macros)

- **Mithril Macro**: mines Mithril and Titanium along your route in the Dwarven Mines
- **Dwarven and Glacite Commission Macros**: read your commissions, travel, complete them and claim the rewards
- **Gemstone Macro**: mines chosen gemstones on a Crystal Hollows route, timing Mining Speed Boost to vein arrivals
- **Powder Macro**: explores hardstone, opens powder chests and can hop lobbies for 2x Powder
- **Tunnel Miner** and **Dynamic Tunnel Miner**: Umber, Tungsten and gemstones in the Glacite Tunnels, with or without a saved route
- **Mineshaft Macro**: runs your Mineshaft Helper plan inside a Glacite Mineshaft, watching the cold
- **Excavator Macro**: fossil solver or treasure focus, with Suspicious Scrap rebuys and fossil salvaging
- Ore, Obsidian, End Stone, Ender Node and Frozen Treasure macros

### [Farming](https://polinexclient.com/farming-macros)

- **Farming Macro**: all 12 Garden crops, with built-in patterns, custom key patterns or waypoints
- **Visitor Macro**: buys or crafts what visitors ask for within a per-visitor budget
- **Garden helpers**: Pest Cleaner, pest traps and loadouts, Sprayonator, Auto Composter, Auto Greenhouse and Chip Planner hand off and return to your lane

### [Combat and slayers](https://polinexclient.com/combat-macros)

- **Enderman Macro (Voidgloom)**: hit shields, laser phases and beacons, melee or Terminator
- **Revenant, Sven and Tarantula Macros**: the full Maddox quest loop, including Revenant Tier V
- **Diana Macro**: finds and digs griffin burrows, answers the Sphinx and fights the Mythological creatures
- Zealot, Ghost, Goblin and Glacite Walker macros

### Fishing and foraging

- **Fishing Macro**: water or lava, with travel, sea-creature combat and hotspot hunting
- **Trophy Fishing Macro**: targets the Trophy Fish tiers you are missing on the Crimson Isle
- **Stridersurfer Macro** for the Galatea cage
- **Galatea Tree, Berry and Mudworm Macros** on Galatea and in Torrhus Canyon

### Rift

Timite, Berberis, Agaricus Cap, Living Metal, Caducous, Sun Gecko, Volt and Mirrorverse Dance macros.

### Progression

- **Attribute Maxer**: ranks shard upgrades by value, then buys and syphons to your target
- **Museum** and **Talisman Macros**: fill donations and Magical Power cheapest-first within a budget
- **Experiment Macro**: Chronomatron, Ultrasequencer and Superpairs
- Minion, Alchemy, Pelt and Carnival macros

## Client features

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Routes** | Record and edit routes in game with `/routeedit`. Imports Polinex, ColeWeight, Nebula, Polar and Melody formats. |
| **Failsafes** | 12 detectors with simple or advanced modes, recorded or generated reactions, and alerts. |
| **Scheduler and Setups** | Run macros with breaks and reconnects, and equip saved hotbar and wardrobe setups before each one. |
| **Macro HUD** | Status, session time, live profit graphs and level ETAs for the running macro. |
| **ESP** | Gemstones, Titanium, frozen corpses, pests, fairy and enigma souls, players and custom blocks. |
| **Visuals** | Custom chat, hotbar and scoreboard, freecam, freelook, zoom, custom sky and themes. |
| **Background running** | Mouse ungrab, keep focus, mute and No Render to keep the game light while a macro runs. |
| **Scripting** | JavaScript and TypeScript scripts with an in-game editor, a script hub and private sharing. |
| **AI Assistant** | Ask Claude Code or Codex from chat with `@polinex`, with every settings change listed so you can undo it. |

Every feature has its own page at [polinexclient.com/features](https://polinexclient.com/features), and setup guides are at [polinexclient.com/guides](https://polinexclient.com/guides).
